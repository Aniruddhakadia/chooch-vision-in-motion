Big questions. A clearer view of what comes next. This is Chooch AI.

What if your shelves could tell you what they need?

Chooch AI turns shelf images into approximate inventory counts—using the shelves you already have, without barcode scans or RFID tags.

When stock runs low, replenishment signals follow. Real-time visibility replaces yesterday’s guesswork.

Can visibility scale without compromising privacy?

No patient data is captured; identifying features are blurred on-device. Chooch is HIPAA compliant, ISO and SOC 2 certified.

Gartner’s May 2026 report describes a rollout across seventeen hundred inventory rooms in forty-one hospitals.

What if service meant availability—not just delivery?

Picture demand based on actual use. Distributors can consolidate orders and plan fewer emergency deliveries.

Managed inventory can then reach hundreds of rooms without expanding manual counting teams.

What could your teams do with time back?

Reported deployments show up to fifty percent higher supply-chain productivity and up to forty-to-fifty percent less inventory.

Critical stockouts fall by up to ninety-five percent; expiration waste by around fifteen percent. Results vary by facility.

Why wait for an empty shelf to sound the alarm?

Resilience starts with knowing what you have. Live consumption trends warn of shortages before shelves empty—even overnight.

Teams can redistribute supplies, source alternatives and locate recalled products with a recorded audit trail.

What changes when both sides see the same reality?

Now the conversation changes: anticipate seasonal demand, agree on substitutes, and measure success by availability at the point of care.

Shared facts build trust—and a stronger basis for pricing and contract discussions.

If inventory gets smarter, what stays in your hands?

Your clinical routines, care decisions, ERP, purchasing contracts and approvals stay yours. Chooch feeds existing systems.

Supply-chain expertise shifts toward exceptions and planning. Less repetitive counting. More time for people.

The right supplies. The right place. The right time. Chooch AI. A healthier tomorrow.
